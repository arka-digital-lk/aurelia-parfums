Est. 2005 · Colombo

Born From a
Passion for Perfection

AURELIA was born in the heart of Colombo, inspired by Sri Lanka's lush spice gardens, tropical blooms, and rich cultural heritage. Our master perfumers blend rare local botanicals — cinnamon, frangipani, and precious oud — with global luxury ingredients to create fragrances that tell the story of our island paradise.
